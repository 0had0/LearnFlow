# LearnFlow
Automate Your Learning Journey

LearnFlow is your personal learning assistant, designed to make knowledge acquisition effortless and efficient. It gathers information from various sources, processes it using smart AI tools, and delivers curated learning content tailored to your interests. Whether you're diving into technical topics, staying updated with research, or expanding your general knowledge, LearnFlow has you covered.

## What Does LearnFlow Do?
LearnFlow is built around three core components:

- Leafs: Gather information from books, articles, blogs, and more.

- Core: Process and summarize content using AI to make it easy to understand.

- Roots: Store the information and turn it into actionable tasks, like scheduled learning goals or flashcards.

## Why Use LearnFlow?
- Stay Organized: Automatically schedule learning tasks to keep your knowledge growing.

- Save Time: No more searching for resources—LearnFlow brings them to you.

- Learn Anything: From technical skills to general knowledge, LearnFlow adapts to your needs.

- Future-Ready: Imagine a learning app where every swipe delivers new, curated content.

## How Can I Use LearnFlow?
- Set up your learning goals and preferred topics.

- Let LearnFlow gather, process, and organize content for you.

- Receive curated learning tasks and materials directly in your workflow.

## Get Started
Ready to automate your learning journey? Clone this repository and follow the simple setup instructions to start using LearnFlow today!
